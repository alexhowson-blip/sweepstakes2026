How the golden boot draw works

Assigned inversely by team seed. The worst-ranked team gets the most likely scorer. Draw Panama, get Mbappé. Draw France, get Désiré Doué at 50/1. Another bite of the cherry — even if your team goes out in the group stages, your player could still be banging them in for someone else's country.

FIFA Peace Prize rules

Cards across the full tournament. Yellow = 1pt, red = 3pts. Two yellows in one game = sending off = max 3pts. Yellow + straight red in the same game = 4pts. Tiebreak: number of reds. Named in honour of the inaugural FIFA Peace Prize, awarded to Donald Trump at the World Cup draw in December 2025. The Village People performed.
